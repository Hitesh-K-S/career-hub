# Job playbook

Target: **AWS Cloud Engineer / SysOps**, with the AI projects as the lever to
move up. Run the checklist on the site as you go.

## Positioning

Most candidates for these roles have a certificate and no running service. Your
position is: *two real applications, live on AWS, with architecture diagrams and
cost numbers.* Lead with that, not with the certificates.

## Target roles

**Apply for:** AWS Cloud Engineer, Cloud Support Engineer, SysOps Engineer,
Junior Backend Engineer (cloud), AI Engineer (junior/applied), MLOps Engineer
(entry).

**Do not apply:** Senior, Staff, Lead, Architect. You will lose on title
filtering and waste referral capital.

**Locations:** Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, remote India.
**Floor:** INR 6,00,000. **Target:** INR 10,00,000. Do not accept under 6L for
a support role in Bengaluru; it signals a bad negotiation, not a good deal.

## Volume, honestly

- 20 applications a week. Not 5. Most entry-level pipelines are volume-limited.
- 3 referral messages a week, always. "Do you know who is hiring cloud
  engineers at X?" beats a cold application by 3-5x.
- Reply to every comment on your posts within 2 hours. The first hour decides
  reach.
- Small and mid-size companies reply faster than large ones and care less about
  credentials. Prioritise them.

## The three interviews, rehearsed

Rehearse out loud, on camera, once a week. Not in your head.

1. **Walk me through your best project.** Use the AccoSpark architecture. Three
   minutes, then stop talking and let them ask.
2. **Live scenario.** "A page is slow - how do you debug it?" Have an ordered
   answer ready. Most people start guessing; have a sequence.
3. **Behavioural, STAR.** Situation, Task, Action, Result. Prepare six. Reuse
   them; do not invent new ones each time.

## Question bank

- What is the difference between a security group and a NACL?
- How would you find out why a Lambda is timing out?
- S3 or RDS for this workload - justify it.
- How do you keep costs under control on someone else's AWS account?
- What happens when an EC2 instance fails?
- How do you store and rotate a database password?
- Explain eventual consistency.
- How would you make this deployment zero-downtime?
- What would you monitor first on a new service, and why?
- How do you debug a slow API endpoint, in order?

## After every interview

Log the company, role, stage and date in the Jobs tab. Then write down three
things you were asked. If one of them exposed a gap in your portfolio, fix the
portfolio and make a LinkedIn post about it. That post will do more for you than
the interview did.

Repeat the same mistake and you deserve the same result.
